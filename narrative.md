## TL;DR

I ran five AI-assisted development processes on real work and kept one. This
talk shows all five on screen with their actual artifacts: what each produced,
and the specific way four of them fell over — too loose (unreviewable), too
heavy (stopped opening it), wrong shape (never fit my work).

The common defect in every process I dropped: it made the decisions that are
mine to make. The one I kept writes down what I decide. You leave with one
test — does this process encode my preferences, or replace them? — and the
process I run today, in enough detail to run it yourself.
